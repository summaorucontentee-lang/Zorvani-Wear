Privacy Policy (Demo)

100% Client-Side Privacy: This site stores cart and order data in your browser's localStorage only. No data is sent to any remote server or backend database.

1. Data We Do Not Collect

Zorvani Wear has no backend API, no tracking databases, no user accounts, and no authentication servers. We never receive, store, or sell:

  • Payment card details or UPI IDs.
  • Personal email addresses or passwords.
  • GPS or physical device location.

2. Local Storage Usage

Your browser's native localStorage API is utilized solely to maintain:

  • zorvani_cart: Active items in your demo shopping basket so they survive page refreshes.
  • zorvani_orders: Fictional checkout receipts for display on the Demo Order History page.

3. Data Deletion

You can erase all stored demo state at any moment by clicking "Clear Demo Orders" on the Order History page, or by clearing your browser cache.
