About Zorvani Wear (DEMO)

Fictional Demo Platform: Zorvani Wear is an entirely fictional e-commerce demonstration. No real garments are produced, stocked, sold, or shipped.

Our Fictional Story

Conceived as an archetype of contemporary Indian casual luxury, Zorvani Wear embodies refined minimalism, breathable natural fabrics, and timeless silhouettes. Every product in this demo—from our Classic Oxford Shirts to Italian-inspired Loafers—is algorithmically designed to simulate a modern boutique brand experience.

Simulated Craftsmanship

In our fictional atelier, we value clean lines and thoughtful details. Real e-commerce stores often struggle with clunky performance, slow loads, and broken checkout journeys. Zorvani Wear was engineered to demonstrate that high-performance, accessible, and delightful shopping experiences can be built using pure, framework-free web standards: vanilla JavaScript, modern CSS, and lightweight vector graphics.

Technical Showcase

  • 100% vector SVG generated product illustrations.
  • Instant client-side multi-facet filtering and sorting.
  • Persistent client-side shopping cart with zero server dependencies.
  • WhatsApp simulated checkout pipeline.
  • Rigorous automated unit and end-to-end testing coverage.
